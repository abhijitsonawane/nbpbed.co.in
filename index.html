Transforming Aspiring Graduates into Inspiring Educators

A Legacy of Excellence in Teacher Education

Navalbhau Pratishthan's College of Education, Amalner, is a forward-looking institution dedicated to shaping highly competent, ethical, and future-ready educators who can lead transformation in modern classrooms.

Approved by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) and affiliated to Kavayitri Bahinabai Chaudhari North Maharashtra University (KBCNMU), Jalgaon, the college upholds the highest standards of academic excellence and professional integrity.

Our flagship Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) programme is thoughtfully designed to blend strong theoretical foundations with immersive practical training, including an intensive school internship that equips student-teachers with real-world teaching expertise.

Driven by a culture of continuous quality enhancement through IQAC initiatives, the institution proudly holds NAAC accreditation and remains committed to innovation, inclusivity, and excellence in teacher education.
